This Privacy Policy explains how Better Days: Sober Tracker (“Better Days,” “the app,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use the app.

The app allows you to enter information related to your personal progress, such as sobriety dates, goals, check-ins, notes, milestones, or other records you choose to save.

8. Children’s Privacy

Better Days is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not currently use accounts or an external data-collection system, we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through our servers.
